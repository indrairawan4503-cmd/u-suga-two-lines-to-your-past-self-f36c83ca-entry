**Two lines to your past self — the unsaid thing.** Line 1 warns your past self about one specific moment of not speaking — waving from the porch instead of shouting the forgiveness your mother needed to hear. Line 2 traces what that silence cost: twenty years of Sundays lost to a misunderstanding.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the two-line text to `/output/entry.txt` (and a byte-identical copy to `entry.txt` next to the script when the directory is writable).
